## 1. What You Can Do

- Use, copy, change and share MuSemantiQ, for any purpose, including commercial use.
- Fork it and keep your changes private.
- Sell a product built on it.

## 2. What You Have to Do

1. Any copy or changed version must say **Based on MuSemantiQ** and link to https://github.com/Guseyn/MuSemantiQ.
2. A commercial product must show a **Powered by MuSemantiQ** credit on its website or a similar public place, or link to the repository.
3. Forks of your fork, and anything built from its code, language design or API, must credit MuSemantiQ too.
4. You can't use my name to promote your work without my written permission.
5. AI models, bots and datasets may use the project only with a visible credit and a link to the repository. An AI product that uses it must credit it in its output or documentation.

It comes **as is**, without any warranty.

Pull requests are not accepted. Issues, suggestions and discussions are welcome.

## 3. What a Credit Looks Like

A line in your footer, about page or documentation is enough:

```html
Powered by <a href="https://github.com/Guseyn/MuSemantiQ">MuSemantiQ</a>
```

A fork says it in its README:

```text
Based on MuSemantiQ — https://github.com/Guseyn/MuSemantiQ
```
